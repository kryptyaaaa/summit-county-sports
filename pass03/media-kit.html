PASS03 / Independent music & culture. A concise fact sheet for artists, promoters, publicists and venue teams.

THE OUTLET.

PASS03 is an independent digital publication dedicated to live concert photography, original music reporting and artist culture. Founded in 2026, with initial original coverage in Northeast Ohio and a long-term multi-city editorial vision. We are an emerging outlet, not an established national magazine.

EDITORIAL FOCUS.

Documentary concert photography; original photo essays and show recaps; emerging-artist profiles; on-record interviews; music culture and fan experiences. Our focus is original editorial work rather than paid promotional placement.
