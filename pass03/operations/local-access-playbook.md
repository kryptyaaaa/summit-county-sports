# PASS03 Local Concert Access Playbook
_2026-10-15 · Operating procedure / not a credential approval. No messages sent._

**Problem:** We want beginner photographers in multiple US cities, including people with no concert experience. PASS03 must support them with *legitimate* local assignments rather than asking them to find their own show or promising media passes.

## 1) Qualify the contributor FIRST
- Real name, home city, age if required by the venue's restrictions, public portfolio, camera gear, accessibility needs, preferred music genres, typical travel radius, and schedule.
- Ask whether they **want** to learn concert photography. Never imply they have to buy a ticket, travel, work for free or use personal funds to join.
- Negotiate a specific assignment, pay/expenses where applicable, deadline, intended publication and limited image-use license **before work**. Photographers keep copyright absent a clear agreement.
- Only name someone on a proposed credential request after they have agreed to the possible editorial assignment and given permission to submit their name.

## 2) Shortlist 3–5 shows near their home address area (not their precise address)
Source published calendars for nearby indie venues, DIY promoters, college venues, independent artists/bands, and daytime/all-ages showcases when suitable. Record:
- Show name, venue, city, date, official event URL, age limits, contact route and photo rules
- Photographer distance and availability, artist interest, whether they need a ticket
- Who authorizes media: artist/tour/publicist, promoter, venue; sometimes all parties
- Camera restrictions: detachable lens, flash, filming, first-three-songs, photo pit, credential pickup

**Start with artist/venue-approved LOCAL SHOWS**, not arena tours. The first assignment is to prove reliability and build a real gallery; media credentials are not guaranteed.

## 3) Three routes to actual access

**Route A — local opening band or independent show (best first step)**
Photographer identifies a favorite local act → PASS03 asks the band's contact if interested in original editorial photo coverage → verify venue/promoter also allows professional equipment and what access is permitted → receive written confirmation of guest list/ticket and camera access → assign photo essay. Artist approval alone may not supersede venue policy.

**Route B — relationship with an indie venue/promoter**
PASS03 introduces itself as a new publication with real published coverage; asks the venue who handles media and which local shows are appropriate for a beginner → photographer and show selected jointly → approval in writing from any required parties. Long-term aim: become known as a reliable publication that gives credit and publishes promptly.

**Route C — larger tour or festival**
Once contributors have samples, approach artist publicists/management or the festival's press form with a *genuine editorial assignment* specifying the photographer, show and expected article. Do not imply a pass exists until formally confirmed.

## 5) Permission checklist — EVERY approved assignment
- [ ] Photographer has voluntarily accepted actual assignment and agreed terms
- [ ] Correct event and permitted photographer NAME confirmed with organizer
- [ ] Written approval for entry or an actual ticket identified (press access does NOT automatically include admission)
- [ ] Camera and lens policy explicitly confirmed
- [ ] Shooting spot/pit permissions and first-three-songs restrictions confirmed
- [ ] No-flash / video / backstage / interview restrictions confirmed
- [ ] Where to collect credential and who to call if not on list
- [ ] Date, ages/ID restrictions, travel and safety considered
- [ ] Clear deliverable, caption, credits and licensing agreed
- [ ] Editorial story published with accurate attribution after fact check

Statuses: **Research → Interested contributor → Assignment proposal agreed → Show selected → Request reviewed by founder → Request sent → Written permission obtained → Shoot → Publish.**

Do not move an event from 'requested' to 'approved' without actual written confirmation. Silence is not authorization. No unsolicited email or DM to prospects or venues without explicit founder approval.

## 8) Early goals
Aim to propose 2–3 realistic nearby local shows for each *interested* contributor. Prioritize one successful assignment with clear permissions over ten credential requests. If access is denied, offer remote artist interviews, existing fully licensed photo essays or local music-scene reporting rather than pretending we can guarantee a pass.
