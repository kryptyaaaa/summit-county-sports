## Weekly achievable content slate, even with ZERO concerts

- Monday: **NEXT UP** artist mini-profile, original artwork or licensed artist-approved photo, two short original insights.
- Tuesday: Instagram/TikTok/Shorts vertical clip from artist-approved remote Q&A or commentary; use allowed in-app licensed audio, not ripped songs or copyrighted music videos.
- Wednesday: **03 ON REPEAT** three real song links and brief original explanations; invite artist comment or consent-based joint post.
- Friday: **PIT NOTES** mini-feature, or interview snippet and quote card with accurate verified quotes.
- Weekend: engage with new artists, respond to submissions, seek collaborative story pitches, and update editorial pipeline. A quiet week is okay; do not fabricate content to hit a quota.

### Growth loops

1. Artist submits track → PASS03 editor independently selects a genuine fit → useful original profile → artist may voluntarily share or accept an Instagram Collab post → fans visit PASS03 → more artist submissions.
2. Local photographer suggests a city story → PASS03 agrees clear rights/payment for that specific use → photographer gets prominent byline and social attribution → authentic story reaches local music community → more prospective contributors.
3. An artist interview gives unique firsthand material → write a properly sourced evergreen article with search-friendly artist name/title → share a short licensed highlight → readers discover archives and opt-in newsletter (when actually launched).
4. Spotlight scene guides ("Where emerging ___ artists play in ___") remain accurate, useful and updated, not scraped generic city lists.

### Originality and music rights safeguards

- Never repost creators' clips or photos just because they are public. Obtain explicit publication permission or an applicable license.
- Use platform-provided licensed music in allowed contexts; in-product availability differs across accounts, song choices and commercial use.
- Attribute interviews accurately; retain recordings/notes according to privacy and consent obligations.
- Provide artists a chance to confirm technical names/titles and obvious factual mistakes, but do not offer guaranteed positive coverage.
- Tag paid promotions and keep them distinct from editorial stories.
- Keep a contributor review queue; no fake writers, photographer staff or interviews on the website.

## First-month measurable goals (not guarantees)

- 10–16 original social posts across genres, not 90 generic news posts.
- 4 artist submissions reviewed; 2 real independent editorial artist profiles released if material and permissions support them.
- 2 original remote artist interviews arranged; no claim until booked.
- 1 distinctive contributor photo essay licensed and published when available.

- Establish site analytics and conversion tracking before quoting audience totals.
- Encourage voluntary artist-shared posts rather than buying followers, bots or paid editorial inclusion.
