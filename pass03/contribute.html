Photographers and writers: interested in helping document music culture? Introduce yourself and show us your work.

HOW IT WORKS.

Contributions can be one-off and non-exclusive. You do not need to travel for PASS03: pitch artists from your own city, submit an existing photo essay you own, conduct a remote interview, or report a verified music-culture story. You retain your copyright unless you separately agree otherwise. We discuss image-use licenses, bylines, deadlines, expenses and any compensation before each assignment. There is no promise of media access, payment, future assignments or publication. Do not send unrestricted originals.
